Our mission

Bring freedom to trade service businesses.

Tradies Bureau was started with one goal: take the admin and compliance weight off trade service businesses so they can focus on the work that actually pays.

The problem

Running a trade business means juggling licences, insurance renewals, certifications, and paperwork on top of the actual work. Miss a renewal and it can cost you a job, a fine, or worse.

The fix

Simple, purpose-built tools — starting with the Compliance Tracker — that keep you covered and give you time back, without adding more admin to your day.

What we believe

Simple, always

If it takes a manual to use, it's not for tradies. Everything we build is built to be picked up in minutes.

Customer focused

We build with trade service businesses, not just for them. What you need drives what we build next.

Freedom over admin

Every feature exists to give you back time and peace of mind — that's the measure we hold ourselves to.

Follow along or get involved

We're building this in the open with the trade businesses who use it. Come say hello.
