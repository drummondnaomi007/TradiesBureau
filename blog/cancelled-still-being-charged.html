Cancelled two months ago, still being charged $1,100

We cut off a subscription two months ago. Then I saw $1,100 go out for it.

I'm out on site. I'm on the tools, not at a desk, and I can't keep on top of my emails when I'm not in the office. I can't remember everything, and I can't be bothered cross-checking every bank line against every subscription we've ever cancelled. It drives me up the wall.

Cancelled and stopped aren't the same thing. Nobody's job to check the gap between them, and the only proof you get is a bank statement after the money's gone.

We've got a lot of subscriptions. A heap of portals that don't talk to each other. Money leaves the account and I'm not always sure where it's going.

Nobody's getting fined over this one. But it's the same problem as the insurance and the rego: nobody's watching while you're busy running the business.

Top tip

When you cancel something, put a reminder in your phone for 30 days later to check it's actually stopped. If it hasn't, ring them then, not two months on.
