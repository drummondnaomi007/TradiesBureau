"I've got to do it all myself": it got you this far, here's how to ease off

"I've got to do it all myself." I know plenty of tradies who say it, and I'm in that age group myself.

It's earned. It got you through the 90s, when businesses were going broke left, right and centre. Our parents and colleagues all had a tough time back then, and the ones who made it did it the hard way. I reckon we're in another tough stretch now.

But you're 55-plus. The body's hurting and you can't keep up with the young blokes like you used to. You want off the tools. You also don't want to sit in an office, because that's not who you are.

What keeps you stuck isn't ego. You're paying wages, and maybe your own kids are on the payroll. You're not sure the insurance and everything else keeps ticking over without you, and you don't fully trust the people underneath you yet. You're not sure there's enough money to stop, so you're eyeing an investment property or a few storage sheds to keep income coming. And you want out quietly.

So it's sell, scale back, or hand it to the kids and hope. Whichever you pick, the business has to hold together without you in the middle of it.

Start with the low-value stuff. Pay the insurance on time. Pay the rego on time. Keep the tickets current. Do the CPD. Pass the audits and don't get fined. It's all small, and it's all sitting in your head. Hand it off, and software can help with vetting your subbies too.

You don't have to take it on faith. You want to see it's covered without having to do it yourself. Whoever takes over, whether that's the kids, a manager or a buyer, will want to see it's all in order, and "ask me" isn't a great answer.

Then go fishing in Far North Queensland. Or wherever you actually want to be. Just not the office.
