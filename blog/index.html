The Threads That Unravel a Trade Business

For owners who want their business systemised enough to delegate, scale, or eventually step back, without the loose threads pulling them under.

Nobody's business falls over in one big hit. It comes apart one quiet thread at a time — a renewal date nobody owned, a subbie nobody properly checked, proof you didn't keep because you were sure you'd remember.

This is a collection of real threads, pulled from years of sitting inside trade businesses as a compliance manager, including our own. Some of these we lived through ourselves before Tradies Bureau existed at all.

If you're already on top of everything, good on you. If you recognise one or two of these, you're in exactly the right place. Read them in any order.
