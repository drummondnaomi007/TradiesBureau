One place to see everything that keeps your trade business legally on the tools: licences, insurance, your people, your subbies, safety and your jobs. You get a heads-up before anything lapses.

What it tracks

Built around the things that actually put a trade business at risk if they slip, and set up for Victorian rules.

🛡

Insurance

Public liability, WorkCover, professional indemnity and more. Each policy is checked against what your trade legally needs, not just its renewal date.

📄

Licences & registration

Trade licences with their numbers, your ABN, and certifications like White Cards, all in one dashboard.

👷

Your team

Fair Work statements sent and signed off, fixed-term contract end dates, award and pay checks after each July wage review, plus right-to-work and licence checks.

🤝

Subcontractors

Onboarding and agreements signed online, WorkCover and PI requested, ABN and GST recorded, and a contractor-or-employee check that saves a dated record.

⚠

Safety

Incident records, the 48-hour WorkSafe written notice for notifiable incidents, SWMS deadlines and apprentice supervision ratios.

🔧

Jobs & contracts

Mandatory inspection stages, compliance certificates, waterproofing records, SOPA payment claims, variations, delays and a handover checklist.

New

Share your credentials with one link

Customer or builder asking for your licence and insurance? Stop digging out PDFs. Send them a link instead.

  • Always up to date. The page reads your records each time it's opened, so a renewed certificate shows straight away.
  • Easy to check. Your registration links to the BPC or Energy Safe Victoria register, and your ABN links to ABN Lookup.
  • You choose what's in it. Pick the licences and policies to include, who it's for, and how long it works: 30 or 90 days, until the job's finished, or until you withdraw it.
  • You see who's looked. Opens and downloads are logged against each link, and job and contract pages show what you've shared.
  • Private details stay private. Policy numbers are masked to the last 4 digits.

Copy it, email it, text it, or pop it in your quote when you send it for signature.
