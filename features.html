Recurring deadlines

Win work

Share your credentials

Always current

QR code for the ute

People

Your team and Fair Work

The right paperwork for every employee, without the spreadsheet.

Fair Work statements

Sent and signed off, including the Fixed Term Contract Information Statement, with a reminder when a contract is ending.

Award and pay checks

Record each employee's award and classification. The digest flags pay not checked since the last 1 July wage review.

Checks per person

Right to work, driver licence, Working with Children, White Cards and high-risk work licences.

Apprentices

Supervision ratios at a glance, so every apprentice has the supervision they need.

Subcontractors

Subbies who upload their own paperwork

Invite a subbie and they send their certificates through their own link.

Checked before it counts New

Uploads wait for you to check them. Accept, or reject with a reason and the subbie gets a link to send it again.

Your own requirements

Choose the cover your subbies must hold, including WorkCover and professional indemnity, on top of what their trade legally needs.

Contractor or employee?

A check based on the Fair Work Ombudsman's indicators, saved as a dated record, with possible super liability flagged.

ABN, GST and payment claims

ABN and GST on file, subbies rechecked every night, and security of payment claims tracked with their deadlines.
