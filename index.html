What tradies actually want

  1. To get paid properly.

    Every dollar you’ve earned for the work you’ve done. Not “close enough”, and not just the bits you remembered to chase.

  2. To not get blindsided.

    No surprise lapse, audit, dispute or fine. You hear about a problem while it’s still a small one.

  3. To be left alone to do the work.

    You got into the trade to work with your hands, not to run an office.

  4. To switch off.

    A holiday, a Sunday night, a sick day, without the phone ringing.

  5. To eventually step back.

    Especially for owners in their mid-50s and beyond. You want to hand over a business that’s still worth something and still running.

What Tradies Bureau gives you

One app keeps track of the paperwork that keeps you legal, and tells you when something needs you. No spreadsheets, no sticky notes, no relying on your memory. Here’s what that’s worth.

  1. Your evenings, weekends and headspace back.

    The paperwork and compliance stop following you home. Sunday night is just Sunday night, and you make better calls on site when you’re not carrying an inbox in your head.

  2. Payment claims that don’t slip.

    Log a payment claim and Tradies Bureau works out when the schedule and the payment are due under Security of Payment rules, then reminds you before those dates pass.

  3. No nasty surprises.

    Tradies Bureau watches the deadlines, licences and obligations so you don’t have to. You hear about a problem early, with what needs doing.

  4. Trust that’s already proven, in one push of a button.

    Builders and other trades want to know you’re properly licensed and insured before they’ll work with you. You can share your credentials and insurance in a tap.

    Builders and head contractors

    See you’re current, so you get on the tools faster.

    Customers

    Can ask for proof and get it straight away, which says a lot about how you run your business.

    Your own subbies

    Are checked too. You know their credentials and insurance are in place before they set foot on site.

  5. A business that runs without you holding it up.

    When the systems and records are in order, the business stops depending on your memory and your hours. That makes a holiday possible now and a sale or handover possible later, with something worth handing over.

Set up in one sitting
