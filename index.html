What Tradies Bureau does for you

One app for the paperwork that keeps you on the tools.

Your compliance map

Everything that applies to your business, your people and your jobs, marked met, missing or due soon. Tick "Doesn't apply" with a reason when it doesn't.

Reminders before it lapses

A Sunday email with what needs you this week, and a WhatsApp message for anything critical. Text "check" any time for a live summary.

One live credentials link

Send customers and builders a link to your registration and insurance instead of PDFs. Renewals show straight away. Add the QR code to your ute.

Your team and your subbies

Fair Work statements, award checks, White Cards and licences for staff. Subbies upload their own certificates, and you check them before they count.

Tenders, contracts, handover

Invite trades to quote on a work package and see who's compliant. Sign contracts and variations online, and collect end-of-job certificates.

Set up for Victorian rules

BPC and Energy Safe Victoria registrations, WorkSafe, the Domestic Building Contracts Act and home warranty, kept up to date for you.

Get going in three steps

Most businesses are set up in one sitting.

  1. Check what applies

    Answer questions about your trades, the work you do, your staff and whether you engage other trades. The map fills in as you go.

  2. Create your account

    Enter your name and email. We send a sign-in link, so there's no password. Your answers become your checklist.

  3. Add what you hold

    Upload your licences and policies. Photos work: we read the expiry date off the certificate for you. Then we keep watch.
