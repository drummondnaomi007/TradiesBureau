Get going in three steps

Most businesses are set up in one sitting.

  1. Check what applies

    Answer questions about your trades, the work you do, your staff and whether you engage other trades. The map fills in as you go.

  2. Create your account

    Enter your name and email. We send a sign-in link, so there's no password. Your answers become your checklist.

  3. Add what you hold

    Upload your licences and policies. Photos work: we read the expiry date off the certificate for you. Then we keep watch.
