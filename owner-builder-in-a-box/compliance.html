1. Eligibility

Under section 25E of the Building Act 1993, the BPC can only give you consent if all of these are true.

2. Certificate of consent

You need one before a building permit can be issued. It's required when the domestic building work is valued over $20,000. That threshold rose from $16,000 on 1 July 2026.

Your own labour counts.

The value is your estimate of labour plus materials plus GST, as if you paid someone to do it all. DIY work can pass $20,000 even when the materials don't.

3. Site, planning & design

Get this right and the rest of the build gets easier. Most expensive mistakes start here.

4. Building permit & pre-start

You appoint the relevant building surveyor (RBS), council or private. The RBS issues the permit and inspects the work.

5. Health & safety (WorkSafe Victoria)

Once trades are on your block it's a workplace. You have duties under the OHS Act 2004 and OHS Regulations 2017.

10. Records & selling

Selling within 6 years 6 months of completion?

Under s137B of the Building Act 1993 it's an offence to sell unless you have (a) a defects report from a prescribed building practitioner, no more than 6 months old, on the BPC template, and (b) for work over $20,000, you've met the statutory insurance scheme requirements, now the First Resort Home Warranty Scheme from 1 July 2026, before signing a contract of sale. The buyer gets copies.
