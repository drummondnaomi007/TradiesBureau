Project management for owner-builders

Eight gateways. No surprises.

Professional builders and government projects use gateways: fixed points where you stop, check you're ready and make a go / hold / stop decision before spending the next chunk of money. The app runs your build the same way.

G0Feasibility G1Concept & budget G2Design & approvals G3Procurement G4Site & base G5Frame & lock-up G6Fit-out G7Handover
How a gateway works

Every gateway has entry criteria (what must be true before you start), deliverables (what you produce) and exit checks (what must be true before you pass). At each gate you make one of three decisions: Go (proceed), Hold (fix the gaps and re-review) or Stop (rethink scope, budget or approach). In the app, a gate won't close until its exit checks are ticked.

G0

Feasibility & eligibility

Can I, should I, and can I afford to owner-build?

Entry criteria

  • You own, or are buying, the land
  • You have a rough idea of the home you want
  • You know how much time you can commit each week

Deliverables

  • Eligibility self-check (s25E)
  • Title, zoning & overlay review
  • Order-of-magnitude budget ($/m² × area)
  • Finance conversation with an owner-builder-friendly lender
  • Honest skills & time assessment

Exit checks

  • Eligible, or exemption pathway known
  • No title or planning showstoppers
  • Budget and finance roughly align
  • Household agrees on time, stress and risk
Decision: Go to concept, or Stop and consider a builder or part-build instead. ~0–1% of budget spent
G1

Concept & budget

Lock the brief, the concept and the money.

Entry criteria

  • G0 passed
  • Designer or architect engaged
  • Feature & level survey and soil test ordered

Deliverables

  • Written design brief (rooms, sizes, must-haves, nice-to-haves)
  • Concept plans & room schedule in My Build
  • Elemental cost plan with 10–15% contingency
  • Finance pre-approval
  • Draft program (target start & finish)
  • First risk register

Exit checks

  • Concept fits the budget, including contingency
  • Concept fits the site (setbacks, overlays, fall, BAL)
  • Scope frozen: changes after this cost money
Decision: Go to detailed design, or Hold and value-manage the concept. ~2–3%
G2

Design & approvals

Everything you need for a building permit.

Entry criteria

  • G1 passed, scope frozen
  • Owner-builder eLearning & white card done

Deliverables

  • Working drawings & specification
  • Engineering: footings, slab, frame, drainage
  • 7-star energy report
  • BAL assessment (if bushfire prone)
  • Planning permit (if required)
  • Certificate of consent from the BPC
  • Legal point of discharge, easement consents

Exit checks

  • All documents consistent (drawings = engineering = energy)
  • Certificate of consent issued
  • RBS appointed and fee agreed
  • Cost plan updated to the detailed design
Decision: Go to procurement, or Hold for design changes. ~5%
G3

Procurement & permit

Right trades, right price, right paperwork. Ready to start.

Entry criteria

  • G2 passed
  • Trade packages defined with a scope for each

Deliverables

  • RFQ packs issued (3 quotes per package)
  • Tender comparison & recommendation per package (tool)
  • Signed trade contracts (guide)
  • Trade register: licences, insurance, SWMS
  • Building permit issued
  • Asset protection permit, insurances, site set-up plan
  • Baseline program & budget

Exit checks

  • At least 80% of the budget is committed under contract or firm quote
  • Every early trade has compliant paperwork
  • Permit, consent & insurances in place
  • Finance drawdown schedule agreed with the lender
Decision: Go to site, or Hold until the price and paperwork are right. ~8%
G4

Site works & base

Out of the ground: set-out, services, footings and slab.

Entry criteria

  • G3 passed
  • Before You Dig done, site fenced, sign up, toilet on site

Deliverables

  • Surveyor set-out
  • Site cut, drainage & underslab plumbing (PCC)
  • Hold point: footing / pre-slab inspection
  • Slab poured, termite management installed
  • Site diary & photos of everything before it's covered

Exit checks

  • RBS inspections passed and certificates filed
  • Engineer's sign-off (if required)
  • Cost to date within ±5% of the baseline
Decision: Go to frame. ~15–20%
G5

Frame & lock-up

Structure, roof, windows and doors. Weather-tight and secure.

Entry criteria

  • G4 passed
  • Frame, truss & window orders confirmed against the plans

Deliverables

  • Wall frames, trusses, bracing, tie-downs
  • Hold point: frame inspection (plus engineer if required)
  • Roof, gutters, fascia, sarking
  • Windows & external doors, wrap, cladding
  • Services rough-in: plumbing, electrical, data, HVAC

Exit checks

  • Frame inspection passed
  • Lock-up reached (lender progress draw)
  • Rough-in certificates, insulation installed & photographed
Decision: Go to fit-out. ~45–55%
G6

Fit-out & fixing

Plaster to paint, wet areas, kitchen, fit-off.

Entry criteria

  • G5 passed
  • All selections locked in My Build (tiles, taps, appliances, paint)

Deliverables

  • Plaster, cornice, internal doors, architraves
  • Waterproofing (certificate) & tiling
  • Cabinetry, benchtops, joinery
  • Painting
  • Plumbing & electrical fit-off (PCC & Certificate of Electrical Safety)
  • Floor coverings

Exit checks

  • All compliance certificates collected
  • Own defects walk-through done, list issued to trades
  • Budget forecast to complete is confirmed
Decision: Go to final. ~85–90%
G7

Completion & handover

Occupancy, close-out and your handover file.

Entry criteria

  • G6 passed
  • Defects closed out, site cleaned

Deliverables

  • Hold point: final inspection
  • Occupancy permit / certificate of final inspection
  • Utilities, driveway, landscaping, asset bond refund
  • Handover file: permits, certificates, warranties, as-builts
  • Final cost report & lessons learned

Exit checks

  • Occupancy permit issued before move-in
  • All trades paid, retentions released per contract
  • Warranty & defects-period reminders set
Decision: Close the project. Welcome home. 100%

Project management principles, owner-builder sized

The essentials of PMBOK and PRINCE2, stripped back to what matters on a single house.

△

Scope · Time · Cost · Quality

Change one and the others move. Freeze scope at G1, then manage every change through a written variation with its price and time impact.

☰

Work breakdown structure

Break the house into trade packages, then tasks. Every task has an owner, a duration, a cost and a predecessor.

→

Critical path

Slab → frame → roof → lock-up → plaster drives the finish date. Protect those trades. Everything else fits around them.

⚠

Risk register

List each risk, its likelihood × impact, the owner and the response. Review it weekly. Contingency is money set aside for these risks, not for upgrades.

$

Cost control

Track Budget → Committed (signed) → Actual (paid) → Forecast to complete per package. React when the forecast moves more than 5%.

✔

Quality: hold points & ITPs

Inspection and test plans for each trade. Nothing gets covered until its hold point is signed off: RBS, engineer or your own checklist.

Starter risk register

RiskLikelihoodImpactResponse
Site costs blow out (rock, fill, poor soil, services)MedHighSoil test and feature survey before G1. Get a site-works provisional sum with a cap.
Key trade unavailable, program slipsHighMedBook critical-path trades at G3. Keep a backup quote for each.
Trade insurance or registration lapses mid-jobMedHighTrack expiry dates in the trade register with automatic reminders.
Failed inspectionMedMedRun your own ITP checklist before calling the RBS.
Scope creep (upgrades)HighMedFreeze scope at G1. Every change goes through a written variation.
Owner-builder burnout or injuryMedHighRealistic program, personal accident cover, a mentor at each gateway.
Wet weatherHighLowAllow weather days in the program. Prioritise lock-up.
