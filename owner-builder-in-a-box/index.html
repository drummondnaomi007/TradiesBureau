Who it's for

Our ideal customer wants to build their own house themselves, and wants to do it properly.

JS

"Jordan", the hands-on owner-builder

35–55 · Regional or outer-metro Victoria
Owns a block · Intends to live in the home

Budget $450k–$900k

Builds 12–24 months

Goals

  • Save the builder's margin (often 15–25%) and put it into the home
  • Control quality, finishes and the pace of the build
  • Do some of the work themselves: project management, carpentry, painting, landscaping
  • Finish on budget and pass every inspection first time

Frustrations

  • Doesn't know what they don't know: permits, insurance, OHS, contracts
  • Quotes all look different and can't be compared fairly
  • Plans, notes and specs are scattered across email, paper and the ute dash
  • Worried about the lender, the surveyor and selling later

Jobs to be done

  • "Tell me exactly what I need, and when"
  • "Help me pick the right trade, not just the cheapest"
  • "Give me the room sizes and spec on my phone at Bunnings"

Where we find them

  • BPC owner-builder eLearning and white card courses
  • Owner-builder Facebook groups and forums
  • Land estates, kit-home and shed suppliers, construction lenders

How it works

The app walks you through the build one gateway at a time.

  1. Check you're eligibleAnswer a few questions. We tell you whether you need a certificate of consent and what comes next.
  2. Set up your buildAdd your block, rooms, plans and budget. Your checklist builds itself.
  3. Package the tradesSplit the work into trade packages with a scope for each one.
  4. Tender & compareSend RFQs, level the quotes and score them. Contract the winner.
  5. Build through the gatesBook inspections, collect certificates and pass each gateway.
  6. Close outOccupancy permit, compliance certificates and a handover file ready for any future sale.
