Tender processing & comparison

Don't pick the cheapest quote. Pick the best one.

Quotes never arrive like-for-like. Run a simple, fair tender for each trade package: check compliance, level the prices, score the rest, and let the numbers make the call.

Jump to the comparison tool

The tender process

The same steps a commercial builder follows, sized for one house.

  1. Define the packageScope, drawings (with revision numbers), spec, program and site conditions.
  2. Build the RFQ packOne pack sent to every tenderer, so every price is for the same thing.
  3. Invite 3–5 tradesSet a closing date (7–14 days). Log each invitation in the tender register.
  4. Answer questionsSend every answer to all tenderers as an addendum.
  5. Close & openRecord each price as received. Late quotes are noted.
  6. Compliance gatesRegistration, insurance, ABN, SWMS, scope. A fail means excluded.
  7. Level the quotesPrice the exclusions, normalise provisional sums, add risk allowances.
  8. Score & decideWeighted scoring, then a written recommendation. Contract the winner and thank the others.

What goes in an RFQ pack

  • Cover letter: package, address, closing date, contact
  • Scope of works + inclusions list
  • Drawings & specification (with revision numbers)
  • Engineering & energy report extracts
  • Program: target start and duration
  • Site info: access, power, water, slope, photos
  • Your draft trade agreement (see Contracts)
  • Pricing schedule so every quote breaks down the same way
  • What to return: licence, insurance, ABN, SWMS, references

Levelling: making quotes comparable

  • Exclusions: if Quote A excludes rubbish removal, add what it will cost you
  • Provisional sums: replace each tenderer's allowance with the same figure
  • Scope gaps: price anything missing against the RFQ
  • Risk: add an allowance for vague or heavily qualified quotes
  • GST: compare everything inc. GST
  • Result: a levelled price for each tenderer, like-for-like

Red flags

  • Price more than 15% under the median: something is probably missing
  • Deposit request above the statutory cap
  • No written quote, or "cash price"
  • Registration or insurance in a different name
  • Long exclusions list or "subject to" everywhere
  • Can't provide references or recent jobs
  • Pressure to sign immediately

How the scoring works

  • Compliance gates are pass / fail. A tenderer who fails any gate (not registered, no current insurance, no SWMS, non-conforming scope) is excluded from ranking, whatever the price.
  • Levelled price = quoted price + cost of exclusions + provisional sum adjustment + risk allowance.
  • Price score = lowest compliant levelled price ÷ this levelled price × 10. The cheapest scores 10.
  • Non-price criteria are scored 0–10 by you: experience, program, quality, communication, warranty.
  • Weighted total = Σ (score × weight) ÷ 10, out of 100. Weights must add to 100%.
  • Quotes more than 15% below the median levelled price are flagged Check scope.
