Signing in

Get into Tradies Bureau

The app lives at portal.tradiesbureau.com. Sign in with a link sent to your email. There's no password to remember.

Already have an account?

Enter your email and we'll send you a sign-in link. If you work in more than one business, you'll pick which one after you sign in.

Sign in

New to Tradies Bureau?

Start with the Compliance Checker. It shows everything that applies to your business, and creating your account from there saves your answers as your checklist.

Check what applies to you

Link expired or not arriving?

Ask for a new one from the sign-in page. Check your junk folder for an email from Tradies Bureau. Still stuck? Get in touch.

Got a link from a tradie or builder?

You don't need an account. Open the link they sent: it shows their current registration and insurance, a quote to respond to, or quotes shared with you.
