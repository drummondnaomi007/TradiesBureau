# Governance & Compliance for Trade Businesses in Victoria (as at 25 September 2026)

Research brief for Tradies Bureau. Covers the Building and Plumbing Commission (BPC) regime and the specific questions raised: plumbers' warranty insurance, building-designer PI, Energy Safe apprentice ratios, WorkCover declaration dates, the domestic contracts reform start date, and the home warranty premium deadline.

## 1. The Building and Plumbing Commission (BPC)

**Start date: 1 July 2025.** The BPC merged three bodies into one regulator [Primary]:
- the Victorian Building Authority (VBA)
- Domestic Building Dispute Resolution Victoria (DBDRV)
- VMIA's domestic building insurance business

It registers and licenses building practitioners and plumbers, resolves domestic building disputes, and (from 1 July 2026) is the **sole insurer** of domestic home warranty.

- Existing VBA registrations and licences moved across automatically and stay valid until the expiry date on the card. [Secondary]
- Online lodgement (including plumbing compliance certificates) still runs through **VBA360**. [Primary]

### Buyer Protections reforms — commenced 1 July 2026
Source: Building Legislation Amendment (Buyer Protections) Act 2025.

| Reform | What it means for trade businesses | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Home Warranty (first-resort) insurance** | Replaces last-resort DBI for contracts signed on or after 1 July 2026. The owner can claim without first having to show the builder is dead, insolvent or disappeared. Cover is up to **$400,000** per home for work over **$20,000** on buildings up to 3 storeys: **6 years** for major defects and **2 years** for other defects. The private DBI market closed. | [Primary] |
| **Rectification orders** | The BPC can order builders, subcontractors and (for multi-storey work) developers to fix incomplete, defective or non-compliant work, up to **10 years** after the occupancy permit or completion. Orders can reach work completed before 1 July 2026. | [Secondary] |
| **Serious defects in apartment buildings** | The BPC can block the occupancy permit, the registration of a plan of subdivision, or settlement. | [Secondary] |
| **Minimum Financial Requirements (MFR)** for domestic builders | Replaces the DBI insurer's eligibility check. For Maximum Construction Capacity up to $20m, adjusted net tangible assets must be at least **5%** of capacity. Above $2m capacity, the financials must be prepared or reviewed by an independent accountant. A builder must notify the BPC **within 5 business days** of becoming aware it can't pay its debts as they fall due. | [Primary] |
| **Developer bond** | **2%** of build cost for residential apartment buildings over 3 storeys, held for at least 18 months after occupancy. Applies to building permits issued after **1 July 2027**. | [Primary – planning.vic.gov.au] |

### Coming next
- **Building and Plumbing Administration and Enforcement Act 2026.** Royal Assent 19 May 2026. Commences mid-to-late 2027, **no later than 1 December 2027**. [Primary – planning.vic.gov.au]
  - Adds director and officer liability and anti-phoenixing powers.
  - Raises the *major domestic building contract* threshold from **$10,000 to $20,000**. Until then, $10,000 is still the threshold.
- **Building manuals.** Required for new Class 1b, 2 and 3 buildings from **1 February 2027**. [Secondary]
- **CPD.** No mandatory CPD scheme for builders or plumbers was found. [Unconfirmed]

## 2. Plumbers — insurance, compliance certificates, supervision

### Insurance
The legal basis is two Ministerial Orders dated 20 June 2002: the *Licensed Plumbers General Insurance Order* and the *Type B Gasfitting Work Insurance Order*. [Primary]

| Cover | Minimum | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Public & products (completed work) liability | **$5,000,000** any one occurrence | Also required for Type B gasfitting |
| Warranty / "all other liability" (defective or incomplete work, consumer claims) — **domestic** | **$50,000** per compliance certificate | A policy with a *total* cap of $50k does **not** comply [Secondary] |
| Warranty — **non-domestic** | **$100,000** per compliance certificate | |
| Duration | **6 years** after the work is completed | |

Who needs it:
- **Licensed** plumbers must hold it. It is an offence to work without it, and evidence of it is required at licensing.
- **Registered-only** plumbers don't need it.
- There is **no** "$200,000 per claim" figure in the Orders.
- No change to these amounts has been made under the BPC. Media reports from Nov 2025 say claims are failing, so reform is possible.

### Compliance certificates [Primary]
- **When required:** for work valued at **$750 or more**, counting labour, materials and GST whoever supplied them. BPC wording varies between "$750 or more" and "exceeds $750".
- **Required whatever the value:** sanitary drains below ground, Type A gas appliance work, consumer gas piping, and cooling towers.
- **Deadlines:** give the certificate to the customer and lodge it with the BPC (via VBA360) **within 5 days of completing the work**.
- Certificates **cannot be withheld because the customer hasn't paid**.
- Failing to issue or lodge is an offence. It can bring infringement notices or a disciplinary referral.

### Apprentice and unlicensed worker supervision (plumbing)
There is no numeric ratio [Primary; regulation text not checked]. A licensed plumber can supervise only in the classes they are licensed in. The work must be either:
- done in the plumber's presence and at their direction; or
- done to a method the plumber specifies, with the plumber inspecting it, taking into account the risk and the worker's experience.

## 3. Electrical — Energy Safe Victoria (ESV)

### Apprentice supervision ratios
The rules come from the *Requirements for the effective supervision of apprentice electricians*, made under s 39 of the Electricity Safety Act 1998 and reg 507 of the Electricity Safety (General) Regulations 2019. **They have been mandatory since 1 September 2025.** Before then the ratios were only recommendations. [Primary]

| Stage | Supervision | Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| 1st year | **All** electrical installation work under **direct** supervision | **1 supervisor : 1 apprentice** [Primary] |
| 2nd year | More tasks allowed under general supervision; high-risk tasks stay direct | See s 8 [Unconfirmed] |
| 3rd year | More general-supervision tasks, some broad | See s 8 [Unconfirmed] |
| 4th / final year | Most tasks can be under broad supervision | Broad: **up to 5 apprentices** per supervisor [Primary – ESV FAQ] |

- **Mixed groups of apprentices:** section 8 of the Requirements sets the ratios for combinations. ESV says supervisors must use judgement for combinations the table doesn't list.
  - The older recommended ratios (2 under direct supervision, 4 under general) keep appearing in search results. **Don't rely on them.** Read the s 8 table in the ESV Requirements PDF.
- **Scope:** the ratios apply only while the apprentice is doing electrical installation work, not tasks like drilling holes or digging trenches. Apprentices may refuse work if supervision is inadequate.
- **Who is responsible:** the employer, including a Registered Electrical Contractor (REC), and the licensed electrician assigned to supervise.

### Registered Electrical Contractor (REC) [Primary]
- **Registration:** up to 5 years. The nominee (technical supervisor) must be an A-grade licensed electrician.
- **Insurance:** public liability of at least **$5 million**, in the REC's registered name. Contracting without it is an offence.

### Certificates of Electrical Safety (COES) [Primary]
- **Non-prescribed work:** give copies to the customer and other relevant parties **within 1 month** of completion.
- **Prescribed work:**
  - A licensed electrical inspector must inspect within **8 business days** of completion.
  - The certificate is completed and returned **within 4 business days** after inspection.
  - The customer gets a copy **within 16 calendar days** of completion overall.

## 4. Building designers and other practitioners — PI insurance

Source: *Building Practitioners' and Endorsed Building Engineers' Insurance Ministerial Order* (Gazette G26, 1 July 2021, republished by the VBA/BPC). [Primary source document; figures from search extracts]

Minimum PI for **building designers / draftspersons** (architectural, interior and services):
- **$1,500,000** any one claim and in the aggregate, where defence costs are *included* in the limit; **or**
- **$1,000,000** any one claim and in the aggregate **plus $500,000** for defence costs, where defence costs sit *outside* the limit.
- At least one automatic reinstatement.

Since 15 Feb 2020, policies may exclude claims about non-compliant or high-risk external wall cladding. No change has been made under the BPC.

## 5. Domestic building contracts reform — start date

The *Domestic Building Contracts Amendment Act 2025* (No. 36/2025) received Royal Assent on **16 September 2025**. [Primary – CAV/legislation.vic]

**Commencement as enacted:** on a day to be proclaimed, or **1 December 2026 at the latest**. [Secondary, consistent across Maddocks, Norton Rose Fulbright and HIA]

**⚠ A delay has since been legislated.**
- The Consumer Legislation Amendment Bill 2026 was amended in committee "to delay [the] forced commencement date" of the 2025 Act (Hansard, 4 June 2026).
- The resulting Act received Royal Assent around **8 September 2026**.
- HIA reported in August 2026: "HIA wins delay to Domestic Building Contracts amendments".
- **The new default date could not be confirmed.** Some sources still say 1 December 2026. Draft regulations were on Engage Victoria from 18 Aug to 16 Sep 2026.
- **Action:** read the commencement clause of the as-made *Consumer Legislation Amendment Act 2026* on legislation.vic.gov.au and check for a proclamation in the Government Gazette before telling clients a date.

**What changes when it commences** [Secondary]:
- **Deposits and progress payments:** the limits move from the Act into **regulations**. Different limits will apply depending on how much of the work uses modern methods of construction (under 30%, 30–50%, over 50%).
  - Current deposit limits: 10% if under $20k, 5% if $20k or more.
- **Cost-escalation clauses:** allowed only where the contract price is **over $1m** (up from $500k), and capped at **5%**.
- **Builder–developer contracts:** new definition of "developer"; several consumer protections (cooling-off, deposit limits, etc.) no longer apply to them.
- **Preliminary services:** some are removed from the definition of "domestic building work".
- **Variations:** stricter process. Separately, the Consumer Legislation Amendment Act 2026 requires certain variations to be in writing, in English and legible.

Remember: the **$10k to $20k** major domestic building contract threshold is **not** in this Act. It is in the Administration and Enforcement Act 2026 (by 1 Dec 2027).

## 6. Home warranty premium — 10 calendar or 10 business days?

**Answer: 10 BUSINESS days** — or earlier if building work starts first.

- **The rule** [Primary – BPC]: "The builder has to pay an insurance premium to the Building and Plumbing Commission within 10 business days of the contract being signed or before the building works starts."
- **HIA's wording** [Secondary]: payment is due before whichever comes first of *(a)* 10 business days after the contract is signed, or *(b)* the start of building works.
- **Variations:** if a variation takes a contract to **$20,000 or more**, the premium is due within 10 business days of the variation being signed.
- **Penalty** [Secondary]: about **$102,000** for an individual and about **$509,000** for a body corporate.
- **Owner's cover:** the owner is covered even if the builder hasn't paid.

**Contracts signed before 1 July 2026 (old DBI regime)** [Secondary]:
- The builder must not take **any** money, including the deposit, until DBI is in place.
- The builder must give the owner a copy of the policy **within 7 days** of it being issued.
- Maximum penalty: 500 penalty units (individual) or 2,500 penalty units (company). In force since 28 Feb 2024.

## 7. Employer obligations (WorkSafe, SRO, ATO)

### WorkCover (WorkSafe Victoria)
- **Registration threshold** [Primary]: you must register if you pay, or expect to pay, **over $7,500** in remuneration in a financial year. If you employ apprentices or trainees, you must register whatever your payroll. You have **60 days** after first becoming eligible.
- **Annual declaration** (certifying actual rateable remuneration):
  - Official rule [Primary]: due by "the due date on your notice" (renewal pack), through Online Employer Services.
  - **Smaller payrolls (≤ $200k):** by **March 2027** for 2025-26 actuals. The previous cycle's date was **20 March 2026**. [Secondary – brokers]
  - **Over $200k:** by **24 October 2026**. [Secondary]
  - There is **no** "$3m / 30 September" rule.
- **Estimate exceeded:** if actual remuneration exceeds, or is likely to exceed, your estimate by **more than 20%**, tell WorkSafe and give a revised estimate **within 28 days**. (WIRC Act 2013 s 443) [Primary text via AustLII]
- **Penalties for underestimating:** up to the premium shortfall. No penalty is applied under $100, and penalties are reduced for voluntary disclosure.
- **2026-27 premiums:** average rate **1.8%**. The early-payment discount is **3% if paid in full by 1 Oct 2026**. [Primary / Secondary]
- **Contractors:** some contractors are deemed workers, so their pay counts as remuneration.

### Payroll tax (SRO) [Primary]
- **Threshold:** **$1,000,000** per year ($83,333 a month) since 1 July 2025. Unchanged for 2026-27.
- **Rate:** **4.85%**. Regional employers pay **1.2125%**.
- **Phase-out:** the threshold phases out between $3m and $5m in national group wages.
- **Surcharges** apply above $10m and $100m in national wages (the mental health and COVID debt levies).

### Superannuation (ATO) [Primary]
- **SG rate:** **12%**.
- **Payday Super, from 1 July 2026:**
  - Super must reach the fund **within 7 business days** of each payday.
  - The Small Business Superannuation Clearing House **closed** on 1 July 2026.
- **Contractors:** those paid mainly for their labour are employees for SG purposes, even if they have an ABN.

### Taxable payments annual report (TPAR) (ATO) [Primary]
- Due **28 August** each year.
- Required if you are mainly (50% or more) in building and construction and pay contractors for building and construction services.

### OHS (WorkSafe) [Primary]
- **SWMS** required **before** any of the 19 kinds of high-risk construction work, e.g. risk of a fall over 2 m, or working near powered mobile plant.
- **White card:** construction induction in Victoria must be done **face to face**, not online.

### Security of Payment (building and construction)
- **Major amendments commenced 15 April 2026** under the Fairer Payments on Jobsites Act 2025. [Secondary]
  - The "excluded amounts" regime is abolished, so variations, latent conditions and delay costs are now claimable.
  - A monthly entitlement to claim replaces reference dates.
  - Claims can be made up to **6 months** after practical completion or last supply.
  - Payment is due within a maximum of **20 business days** after the claim.
- **Key deadlines under the Act** [Primary – BPC]:
  - Payment schedule: within **10 business days** of receiving a claim.
  - Adjudication application: within **10 business days**.
  - Adjudicator's decision: within **10 business days**, extendable to 30.
