Twelve trades, written out one by one: what the law requires of every business, and what depends on the work you take on. These are the same rules the app checks you against, so this page and the app never disagree.

General information, not legal advice. For the reasoning and the sources behind each rule, read the compliance guide.

Electrician

12 things apply to every Victorian business in this trade. More depend on the work you take on.

Every business in this trade

Licences and registrations

  • Registered Electrical Contractor (REC) RegistrationRequired by law

    Energy Safe Victoria. The business registration needed to contract for electrical work; renews up to every 5 years.

    Checked against the Energy Safe Victoria register.

  • A-grade Electrician LicenceRequired by law

    Energy Safe Victoria. Personal licence; renews every 5 years with CPD.

    Checked against the Energy Safe Victoria register.

Insurance

  • Public Liability InsuranceRequired by law

    A Registered Electrical Contractor must hold at least $5m public liability (Energy Safe Victoria). Contracting without it is an offence.

Tickets and training

  • CPR/First AidUsually asked for
  • White CardUsually asked for

    Construction induction card. Doesn't expire, but must be redone after 2+ years out of construction work. In Victoria the course must be done face to face.

  • Electrical Safety CompetencyUsually asked for

    Checked against the Energy Safe Victoria register.

  • Electrical Testing CertOptional

    Checked against the Energy Safe Victoria register.

Dates and paperwork

  • LeavePlus quarterly returnBy law, if it applies to you

    Construction employers' portable long service leave (called CoINVEST until August 2023). Workers' days and wages due 14 days after each quarter ends; the charge is 2.7% of ordinary pay.

  • Taxable Payments Annual Report (TPAR)By law, if it applies to you

    Payments to contractors for building and construction work. Due 28 August.

  • WorkCover remuneration declarationBy law, if it applies to you

    24 October if last year's wages were over $200,000; otherwise about 20 March. Use the date on your renewal notice.

  • WorkCover premium (pay in full for the discount)By law, if it applies to you

    3% off if paid in full by 1 October (2026-27). Check the date on your renewal notice.

  • Written notice of a notifiable incidentRequired by law

    Notify WorkSafe straight away, preserve the site, and send written notice within 48 hours.

Depending on your work

If your work includes: Electrical installation work
  • Certificate of Electrical SafetyRequired by law

    Copies to the customer within 1 month of finishing.

If your work includes: Prescribed electrical work
  • Certificate of Electrical Safety (prescribed work)Required by law

    Inspected within 8 business days; the certificate within 4 business days of that; the customer's copy within 16 days.

If you work at heights (roofs, scaffolding, elevated platforms, balconies)
  • Working at HeightsUsually asked for
If you operate boom lifts over 11m, forklifts, scaffolding, rigging or dogging
  • High Risk Work Licence (WorkSafe)Usually asked for

    Boom EWP 11m+, forklift, scaffolding, rigging or dogging. Renews every 5 years; sight it before they operate.

If you employ staff or apprentices
  • WorkCoverRequired by law

    Required once you pay more than $7,500 a year in wages, or employ any apprentice or trainee (WorkSafe Victoria). Register within 60 days.

  • Fair Work Information StatementRequired by law
  • Electrical apprentice supervision ratioRequired by law

    From 1 September 2025: a first-year apprentice works under direct supervision, one to one; under broad supervision one supervisor can have up to 5 apprentices.

Depending on your work

If your work includes: Internal wet areas (AS 3740)
  • Waterproofing certificateUsually asked for

    Building surveyors usually ask for it at the final inspection, and it's your evidence if a leak claim comes later. Keep it 10 years.

If your work includes: Balconies, roofs, podiums, planters (AS 4654.2)
  • Waterproofing certificateUsually asked for

    Building surveyors usually ask for it at the final inspection, and it's your evidence if a leak claim comes later. Keep it 10 years.

If your work includes: Basements and below-ground (to the design)
  • Waterproofing certificateUsually asked for

    Building surveyors usually ask for it at the final inspection, and it's your evidence if a leak claim comes later. Keep it 10 years.

If you contract directly with home owners for jobs over $10,000
  • Domestic Builder (Unlimited) Registration (BPC)Required by law

    Needed for domestic building work over $10,000 for home owners. A company is registered separately, with at least one director registered in their own name.

    Checked against the Building and Plumbing Commission register.

  • Minimum financial requirements for domestic buildersRequired by law

    From 1 July 2026 the BPC sets your Maximum Construction Capacity, and your adjusted net tangible assets must be at least 5% of your uncompleted domestic work.

If you contract for commercial work that needs a building permit
  • Commercial Builder Registration (BPC)Required by law

    Unlimited, or limited to one of 11 classes of commercial building work.

    Checked against the Building and Plumbing Commission register.

If you operate boom lifts over 11m, forklifts, scaffolding, rigging or dogging
