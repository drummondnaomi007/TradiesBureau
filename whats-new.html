The latest in Tradies Bureau

Here's what we've added lately. Most of it came straight from tradies and builders telling us what was eating their time.

Late September 2026

The new build: one app for every business

The biggest update yet. Sign-up now starts with the Compliance Checker, and builders can run tenders on compliant trades.

The Compliance Checker

Answer questions about your trades, your work, your staff and whether you engage other trades, and watch a map of everything that applies fill in beside them. Nothing is saved until you create an account, and your answers become your checklist.

Your compliance map

Every requirement for your business, each person and each job, marked met, missing, due within 30 days or doesn't apply. Each record shows how sure we are, from your own upload to a register check.

One sign-in, every business

Everyone signs in as themselves. Work in more than one business and you switch between them. Roles for Owner, Admin, Employee and a read-only Auditor.

Subbie uploads checked before they count

Certificates from subbies wait for you to accept them. Reject one with a reason and they get a link to send it again.

Projects, work packages and tenders

Invite trades to quote from a link with no account. Bidders are checked live against the package, compliant first. Awarding someone missing a legal requirement needs a reason, kept in the audit log.

Share quotes with your client

A read-only page of a package's quotes and each bidder's compliance, with prices shown or hidden, and who you chose.

End-of-job certificates

Subbies send their Certificate of Electrical Safety or plumbing compliance certificate to your handover checklist. Accept it and it's ready for your client.

A free account for trades

Trades who quote through a link can keep their certificates in a free account and share one live link with every builder.

Earlier in September 2026

Credentials, Fair Work, safety and contracts

Share my credentials

Send customers and builders your registration and insurance

One live link instead of a pile of PDFs

Pick what to include (licences, ABN, your latest policy of each type), who it's for, and how long it lasts. Copy, email or text it. Renewals show up straight away and anything expired shows as expired.

Links for a job or contract

Fill a link in from a job or contract. A contract's link can also show the project's own certificates, like its home warranty certificate, including ones added later.

See opens and downloads

Every link you've shared shows when it was opened and what was downloaded, and you can withdraw any link at any time.

Always-on link and QR code

One permanent link for your website, email signature or ute. Change what it shows and the address stays the same.

Your team and Fair Work

Employees, pay and the right paperwork

Fixed-term employees

Record a contract end date. The Fixed Term Contract Information Statement is sent with the other Fair Work statements, and you get a reminder when a contract ends within 30 days.

Award and pay checks

Record each employee's award and classification, with links to Fair Work. The digest flags anyone whose pay hasn't been checked since the last 1 July wage review.

Contractor or employee?

A check on each subcontractor based on the Fair Work Ombudsman's indicators. It saves a dated record and flags possible super guarantee liability.

Checks per employee

Right to work, driver licence, Working with Children and high-risk work licences, all tracked alongside everything else.

Safety

Incidents, notices and supervision

WorkSafe written notices

Tracks the 48-hour written notice for notifiable incidents, and puts any unsent notice on the dashboard's critical list.

Apprentice supervision ratios

See at a glance whether your apprentices have the supervision they need.

SWMS and certificate deadlines

Jobs carry their statutory certificate and SWMS deadlines, so nothing slides at the end of a job.

Subcontractors

Onboarding, insurance and payments

Set your own insurance requirements

Choose what cover your subbies must hold, including WorkCover and professional indemnity, checked against each trade's legal requirements.

ABN and GST recorded

Each subcontractor's ABN and GST registration is on file, and subbies are rechecked every night.

SOPA payment claims

Track security of payment claims from subcontractors, with the deadlines in your weekly digest.

Jobs and contracts

From signing to handover

Sign documents online

Send documents on a job to be signed online, including design and services agreements.

Variations sent automatically

Signed variations go to your client automatically, with a PDF.

Domestic building checks

Claims to the owner are checked against the Domestic Building Contracts Act, and contracts are classed as domestic, commercial or mixed use.

Inspections, waterproofing and handover

The full set of mandatory inspection stages, a waterproofing record, a delays and extension of time register, and a handover checklist.

Everyday polish

Small things that make a big difference

Clearer wording

Plain-English status labels like “Due today” and “3 days left”, and Australian spelling throughout.

Works on any screen

No sideways scrolling on small phones, labels on every form field, and faster page loads.

Recurring business deadlines

LeavePlus, TPAR and WorkCover deadlines come round each year without you setting them up again.
